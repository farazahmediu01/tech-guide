# **Program Overview**

The Certified Agentic AI Architect is a five-quarter professional certification program for AI engineers. It takes you from AI-driven development fundamentals through building and deploying Agent Factories at cloud scale.

The program follows a progressive four-level certification structure. Each level is earned by passing the corresponding quarter’s exams. All four levels culminate in the Certified Agentic AI Architect credential. Critical thinking skills from Part 0 (Thinking is the Curriculum) and hands-on OpenClaw projects run as parallel tracks throughout.

In Quarter 4, students also sit Anthropic’s official **Claude Certified Architect — Foundations (CCA-F)** exam as a supplementary industry certification. This is a proctored, 60-question, scenario-based exam administered by Anthropic that validates production-grade Claude architecture skills. It supplements the internal PIAIC exams and provides students with an externally recognised credential from the maker of Claude.

## **Claude Certified Architect — Foundations (CCA-F)**

Anthropic launched the CCA-F on March 12, 2026 as its first official technical certification, backed by a $100 million investment in the Claude Partner Network. It is a proctored, scenario-based, architecture-level exam that validates whether a practitioner can design and ship production-grade Claude applications at enterprise scale.

In this program, the CCA-F serves as a supplementary certification in Quarter 4 (AI-321). By the end of Q4, students have covered all five CCA-F exam domains through the PIAIC curriculum. The CCA-F adds an external, industry-recognised credential from Anthropic that validates their production architecture skills alongside the internal PIAIC exams.
